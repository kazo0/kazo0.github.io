Daily Reflection is a free, open-source app for Android and iOS that brings you each day's reading from *Daily Reflections*, the book of reflections written by A.A. members for A.A. members.

[Download on the App Store][app-store]{: .btn .btn--primary}
[Get it on Google Play][google-play]{: .btn .btn--primary}

## Features

- **Today's reflection**: open the app to the day's reading, pick any other day of the year, and share a reading with a friend.
- **Secular readings**: switch to readings from *Beyond Belief: Agnostic Musings for 12 Step Life* by Joe C.
- **Sober time**: set your sober date and see how long it has been, in years, months, and days or as a total day count.
- **Daily reminder**: get a notification at the time of day you choose.
